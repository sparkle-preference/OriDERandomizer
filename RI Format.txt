
Slot values
===
{n} is replaced with whatever save slot n holds, so a message can read a counter a
seed keeps for itself. RandomizerItemIDs.txt lists the slots; 8000-8999 and anything
from 20000 up are free for a seed's own use, the difference being that 8000-8999
survives a death. RI and BM take the same {n} on the right of an =.
- "You have found {8000} of them." -> "You have found 3 of them."

RI and BM also take math on the right of an =: numbers (up to two decimals) and {n}
joined by + - * / %, with * / % going first. Division drops the remainder. Math takes no
parentheses. A slot holds whole numbers, so an RI that comes out fractional is rounded,
halves away from zero, with a warning; /1 drops the fraction instead.
- RI|27000={20000}*2 - 1 -> slot 27000 becomes twice slot 20000, minus 1
- RI|8000+={8001}*3 -> slot 8000 goes up by three times slot 8001
- RI|8000={8001}*0.75/1 -> slot 8000 becomes three quarters of slot 8001, fraction dropped

A comparison in parentheses, (a OP b), is 1 when it holds and 0 when it does not: a and
b are math, OP one of == != < > <= >=. AND, OR and NOT (any case) join comparisons, each
in its own parentheses. AND goes before OR, and once the answer is known the rest is skipped.
- BM|4=(5=={8000}) -> box 4 on while slot 8000 holds 5, off otherwise
- BM|4=({8000}>={8001}) -> box 4 on while slot 8000 is at least slot 8001
- RI|8002=({8000}!=0) -> slot 8002 becomes 1 or 0
- BM|4=({8000}>=3) AND NOT ({8001}==0) -> box 4 on if slot 8000 is at least 3 and slot 8001 isn't 0
- RI|8002=({8000}==0) OR (10/{8000}>2) -> never divides by zero

In a multipickup, / is written //: MU|RI/27000={20000}//2/SH/Halved

Boxes
===
Box n is the seed's nth BX line, counting from 0. {Bn} reads 1 while box n is on and 0
while it is off; {Bn.x} and {Bn.y} read its top-left corner. They work anywhere {n} does.

BR moves a box by its top-left corner, keeping its size:
- BR|4=120.5,-40 -> box 4's top-left corner goes to (120.5, -40)
- BR|4+=3,0 -> box 4 moves 3 to the right (-= moves it the other way)
- BR|4 -> box 4 goes back to where its line puts it
- BR|5={B4.x},{B4.y}+10 -> box 5 sits 10 above box 4
Moves are saved with the game and undone by a death, like a box switching on or off. A
moving box doesn't carry Ori with it. The box editors show boxes where their lines put them.
