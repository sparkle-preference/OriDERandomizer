
Slot values
===
{n} is replaced with whatever save slot n holds, so a message can read a counter a
seed keeps for itself. RandomizerItemIDs.txt lists the slots; 8000-8999 and anything
from 20000 up are free for a seed's own use, the difference being that 8000-8999
survives a death. RI and BM take the same {n} on the right of an =.
- "You have found {8000} of them." -> "You have found 3 of them."

RI and BM also take a comparison in parentheses on the right of an =: (a OP b) is 1
when it holds and 0 when it does not, a and b each a number or {n}, OP one of
== != < > <= >=. No arithmetic, and the parentheses are required.
- BM|4=(5=={8000}) -> box 4 on while slot 8000 holds 5, off otherwise
- BM|4=({8000}>={8001}) -> box 4 on while slot 8000 is at least slot 8001
- RI|8002=({8000}!=0) -> slot 8002 becomes 1 or 0
